Privacy Policy

Meet Finn is a trading name of D&R Soutar Investments Pty Ltd (ABN 66 604 539 960). We're based in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

If you have questions, contact us at hello@meetfinn.com.au.

What we collect

When you use the free snapshot

We collect the answers you give to Finn's diagnostic questions. These may include details about your income, debts, savings, insurance, and financial situation. We also collect your email address if you choose to provide it.

When you use the Clarity Session

We collect the same diagnostic information in more detail, plus any documents you choose to upload — including bank statements. You control what you share.

Automatically

We collect standard technical information when you visit the site — browser type, pages visited, time on page. This is used to understand how the site is being used and to improve it. We do not use this to identify you personally.

How we use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Generate your personalised financial snapshot or Clarity Session output
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of Finn's responses over time
  • Send you relevant information if you've opted in to hear from us
  • Connect you with a vetted financial professional if you request it (see Referrals below)

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties except as described in this policy.

Third parties we use

Anthropic — Finn's AI responses are generated using Anthropic's Claude API. Your diagnostic answers are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate your output. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that processing: anthropic.com/privacy

Formspree — We use Formspree to capture email addresses submitted through the site. Formspree's privacy policy applies: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

Netlify — Our site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may collect standard server logs. Netlify's privacy policy applies: netlify.com/privacy

Referrals

If Finn recommends connecting you with a financial adviser, mortgage broker, or accountant, we may receive a referral fee from that professional. This is disclosed to you before any referral takes place. The referral is based on fit, not on the size of the fee. You always choose whether to proceed.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. Bank statements and uploaded documents are used only to generate your output and are not retained beyond that session unless you explicitly save your progress.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Make a complaint if you believe your privacy has been breached

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@meetfinn.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Not financial advice

Finn provides financial clarity and organisation — not financial advice. Nothing on this site or in Finn's outputs constitutes personal financial advice under the Corporations Act 2001. If you need financial advice, Finn can help you find a qualified professional.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be at meetfinn.com.au/privacy. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it was last updated.
